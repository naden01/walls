Drop your wallpaper images into this folder.

Naming convention (must match res/xml/wallpapers.xml):
  wallpaper_1.png
  wallpaper_2.png
  wallpaper_3.png

Requirements:
  - Format     : PNG / JPG / WebP
  - Resolution : >= 1080x2460 (this device), ideally 1440x3200
  - NO spaces in filenames, use underscores

The wallpaper_*.png currently in this folder are throwaway 1080x2460 placeholders.
Replace them, then update:
  1. res/xml/wallpapers.xml  -> one <static-wallpaper> per image (un-namespaced attributes!)
  2. res/values/strings.xml  -> the title string per wallpaper
